**16. Sunday Night Cartoon / Animated Sitcom**

```
Change the app's style to "American Animated Sitcom — Simpsons / Family Guy era". The entire UI is hand-drawn looking — thick uneven black outlines on every element (CSS filter: url(#wobble) SVG turbulence filter to make all edges slightly wobbly and imperfect, simulating a felt-tip pen). Background is a flat cartoon living room — mustard yellow walls, ugly plaid couch, a TV in the corner playing static. Color palette is pure Simpsons: mustard yellow, sky blue, safety orange, flat white — no gradients anywhere, all solid fills like a paint bucket tool.

On load, a cartoon character walks in from the left side of the screen (CSS sprite animation), trips, looks at the camera with a surprised expression, then a comedic BOING sound plays and a speech bubble pops up with the headline text.

Buttons are big, rounded, chunky cartoon shapes — when hovered they SQUASH and STRETCH (classic Disney 12 principles animation: scale Y down, scale X up, then bounce back). When clicked, a cartoon SMACK or POW starburst graphic explodes from the button in yellow and white.

Section transitions use an IRIS WIPE — the old section closes like a camera shutter into a circle, then the new one irises open, exactly like a Looney Tunes scene change.

A laugh track plays on certain interactions. The cursor is replaced with a cartoon gloved hand (Mickey-style four-finger white glove). Background music is a cheesy tuba-heavy sitcom theme. Gravity affects everything — elements don't just appear, they DROP from the top with a rubber bounce. Nothing is serious. Nothing is still. The UI has a personality and it's chaotic and loveable.
```
